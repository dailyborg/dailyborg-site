# workers/truth-engine (dailyborg-truth)

Every six hours reads the PolitiFact fact-check feed, matches the speaker by PolitiFact's own URL slug to our politicians, stores every ruling with its source link, and recomputes trust scores. No model is involved anywhere in this worker, because no language model may decide facts about a real person.

- **Matching is exact or nothing.** A feed slug is compared to our politician slugs on letters and digits only, with a trailing state suffix stripped, so `jd-vance` matches `j-d-vance` and `susan-collins` matches `susan-collins-me`. If two or more of our rows answer to the same slug, nothing is stored for that ruling and one `ingestion_logs` row goes in with status `validation_warning`, naming the feed slug and every candidate. Fix the roster, then the ruling lands on the next run.
- **Spanish republications are dropped on the link.** `isSpanishEdition` reads the URL path only, so no English ruling can ever be skipped for the words it happens to contain. It skips an `espanol` or `es` path segment, a Spanish month in the date segment of a `/factchecks/` URL (`ene`, `abr`, `ago`, `dic`, `set`; the other nine are spelled the same in both languages), and a slug ending in `-es`. An unrecognised month is skipped too rather than filed under an invented January date.
- **Known gap (2026-09-07).** PolitiFact's RSS rewrites a Spanish ruling's month back to English, so a Spanish item whose slug is plain Spanish words carries no marker in the feed link at all. Observed that day: `/factchecks/2026/aug/27/byron-donalds/sarampion-brote-inmigracion-vacuna-florida/` in the feed, whose page answers `<html lang="es">` with a canonical URL of `/factchecks/2026/ago/27/...`, alongside the English original at `/factchecks/2026/aug/27/byron-donalds/us-measles-illegal-immigration-mmr-vaccine-fl/`. That pair is stored twice today and counts twice in the mean. Closing it needs either a second subrequest per item to read the canonical URL, or a same speaker plus same date plus same rating check before the insert. Neither is in yet, both are the Director's call.
- **Trust score.** 100 minus the mean falseness of *every* ruling stored for that official, minimum three rulings, rounded. A `trustworthiness_history` row is written only when the number actually changed.
- **Manual run.** `POST ?action=sync`, at most one every 10 minutes; the lock is an ISO timestamp in `kv_store` under `truth_manual_lock_until`. A GET returns the status line and syncs nothing.

`parseFeed` and `isSpanishEdition` are pure and exported so they can be tested without a Cloudflare binding. Bindings: `DB` (dailyborg-db) only, no secrets. Cron: `40 */6 * * *`.
