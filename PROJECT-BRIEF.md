# PROJECT-BRIEF (draft written by Claude during the 2026-09-05 takeover; Dr. Cato to refine)

## What we are building
The Daily Borg: an autonomous digital newspaper with seven desks (Politics, Crime, Business, Entertainment, Sports, Science, Education) written from wire RSS by an AI writer, plus the Borg Record, a public accountability index of every United States official (federal, state, local) built only from official rosters and published fact-check rulings.

## Audience
Readers who want a fast daily briefing and a place to check what an official actually said and did, with every claim linked to its source. Secondary: subscribers who track specific officials and get alerts.

## Core value
Trust through provenance. Nothing about a real person is generated by a model. Every roster entry and every ruling links to its source.

## Success criteria
- Runs on the Cloudflare free tier with no overage emails.
- Every federal legislator, the President and the Vice President are in the record with correct office, party and state within 24 hours of a change.
- Every state legislature is in the record and refreshed weekly.
- No fabricated data anywhere on the site.
- Daily reporting publishes without a person, capped to control cost.

## Not in v1
Real roll-call votes (needs a congress.gov API key), local officials at scale, premium paid tier (Stripe wired but not enabled), social auto-posting, the blog/SEO pipeline from PROJECT-START section 6, the design pass from section 2.
